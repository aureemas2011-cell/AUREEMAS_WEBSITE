About Us

Committed to Excellence in Electronics

Welcome to Aureemas Electricals & Electronics. We are a dedicated team of professionals specializing in power solutions. With years of experience in the industry, we pride ourselves on delivering robust energy solutions tailored to domestic and industrial needs.

Our core expertise lies in the manufacturing of custom inverters and battery systems, as well as providing top-tier servicing for all major brands. We believe in quality, durability, and customer satisfaction.

🏆 An ISO 9001:2008 Certified Company

Our Products & Services

Inverter Manufacturing

Custom-built inverters designed for high efficiency and longevity. We manufacture standard pure sine wave inverters suited for homes and businesses.

Battery Manufacturing
